# Blocked or externally dependent items

No technical blocker prevents the reproducible synthetic analysis.

The following items require accountable human action before submission:

1. scientific interpretation, normative framing, and final manuscript prose;
2. author names, affiliations, contributions, conflicts, funding, and
   corresponding-author details;
3. confirmation that each empirical source's terms permit the intended public
   redistribution, rather than only local preservation and citation;
4. final recheck of live Conservation Biology and ScholarOne requirements;
5. anonymous archival location for review data/code if the authors choose to
   provide one during double-blind review;
6. accountable approval of the four bounded literature claims supported by
   inspected complete article text.
